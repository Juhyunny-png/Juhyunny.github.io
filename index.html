Language is part of the AI problem.

My expertise lies in fine-tuning LLM prompts and responses, applying linguistic frameworks to LLM data governance, and evaluating model outputs against structured data-pipeline rubrics to ensure response quality. I leverage edge cases and recurring patterns in model responses to investigate the underlying behaviors and parameters influencing response generation, contributing a linguistic perspective to the quality assurance and evaluation of transformer-based model pipelines.

I conduct quality control through structured evaluation metrics and linguistic analysis, tracing observable model failures back to their underlying error taxonomies. I communicate these findings to cross-functional teams to support data curation, guideline refinement, and alignment around ground-truth standards.

My goal is to make agentic AI systems more accurate, contextually appropriate, reliable, and useful across diverse linguistic and user contexts.
