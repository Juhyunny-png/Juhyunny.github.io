01 / About

Language is part of the AI problem.

My work sits at the intersection of linguistics and generative AI. I am interested in understanding not only whether an LLM produces an acceptable answer, but why it succeeds or fails.

I approach model quality through structured evaluation, linguistic analysis, human judgments, error taxonomies, prompt testing, and adversarial examples.

My goal is to make AI systems more accurate, contextually appropriate, reliable, and useful to people.
